# Spektrum A1+ Interactive — v1.1 content status

## Complete book coverage
- 12/12 chapters are present.
- 255 main-course activity cards are present.
- 134 numbered Vertiefung activities are present.
- 46 chapter-test activities are present.
- Source pages 9–271 are integrated in the app, including reading passages, diagrams, tables and visual exercises.
- Start Deutsch 1 and the final structure reference are included.
- All 126 supplied MP3 files are included.

## Vocabulary system — v1.1
- 526 clean German vocabulary/phrase review cards are included from the official Schubert chapter “Wörter und Wendungen” bilingual lists.
- English meanings are complete for all 526 cards.
- Arabic meanings/help are included for all 526 cards and are shown when Arabic Help is enabled.
- Review follows the DeutschPath mechanism: aligned queue, due/new indicators, flip card, Again / Hard / Good, SRS scheduling, pronunciation and shadowing.
- Vocabulary Testing is separate from review; only correct typed Full Recall confirms learned status.

## Contribution / streak — v1.1
- Contribution is based on eight independent daily study sections: Practice, Reading, Listening, Vocabulary, Word Test, Grammar, Writing and Speaking.
- A section contributes at most one heat level per day, regardless of repetitions.
- Vocabulary Review counts under Vocabulary; Vocabulary Testing counts under Word Test.
- Completing/uncompleting/re-completing the same exercise cannot inflate contribution.
- Unmarking an exercise never creates contribution.
- Legacy v1.0 event history is migrated from categories/kinds; old raw point totals are not used to inflate levels.
- Streak uses whether at least one contribution section is active on a day; if today is still inactive, continuity is calculated through yesterday.

## Source limitations
The separate official Lösungsheft was not supplied with the user files, so the app does not invent official answer keys. The supplied audio archive also did not contain the appendix listening recordings for Start Deutsch 1.
