v1.4 vocabulary gap audit and UI alignment

- Added 150 curated gaps: 106 lexical words and 44 high-value Redemittel/phrases.
- Final bank: 1,552 items (1,424 words + 128 phrases/sentences).
- Every added item includes English and Arabic meaning plus source metadata.
- Page-level verification rejects OCR fragments and material that is not present in the integrated study pages.
- Vocabulary Bank header/toolbar now uses the same current compact layout as the other standalone Spektrum apps.
- Full recall is the default Vocabulary Testing mode.
- Missing letters are filled directly inside the word.
- Level badge is B1+ and B1 UI contains no cross-level development labels.
