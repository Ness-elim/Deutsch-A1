ASPEKTE B1+ VOCABULARY GAP AUDIT — v1.4.0

Scope scanned
- Lehrbuch OCR (all available pages)
- Arbeitsbuch OCR (all available pages)
- Lehrbuch transcript
- Redemittel reference

Method
1. Compared the existing v1.3.0 bank against the full text corpus.
2. Used dictionary-form matching and frequency as candidate signals.
3. Manually rejected OCR fragments, proper names, task-instruction boilerplate, duplicate inflections and low-value noise.
4. Added only useful lexical gaps and high-value B1 Redemittel with English and Arabic meanings.
5. Newly discovered items remain candidates and do not enter Vocabulary Testing until approved by the learner.

Counts
- Before audit: 1402 items
- Added after audit: 150
  - lexical words: 106
  - phrases / Redemittel: 44
- Final bank: 1552 items
- Official vocabulary: 792
- Book / material discoveries: 760

Quality rules
- No names or publisher/photo credits were added.
- No isolated OCR fragments were added.
- Inflected forms were consolidated to dictionary forms where possible.
- Nouns include articles when known.
- Ordering keeps the course chapter/appearance sequence; Redemittel items are tied to their referenced chapter.
